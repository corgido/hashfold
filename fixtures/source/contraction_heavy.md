"I don't understand why they won't listen," she said, shaking her head. "It isn't that difficult. They can't keep ignoring the evidence. We shouldn't have to repeat ourselves."

"But it doesn't matter whether they've read them or not. The conclusions aren't controversial. Nobody's disputing the methodology. They're just refusing to engage."

"You're being unfair. They aren't refusing — they haven't had time. There's a difference between can't and won't, and you're conflating the two."

"It isn't pretending. They genuinely don't have the bandwidth. You shouldn't assume malice when the explanation is simpler."

"I'm not assuming malice. I'm observing a pattern. They didn't respond last time either. And they wouldn't give a straight answer when I asked directly. That's not bandwidth — that's avoidance."

"We have tried everything else. We've sent emails. We've scheduled meetings they didn't attend. We've escalated informally and it hasn't changed anything. What's left?"

"Deal. But if they don't take it seriously this time, I'm done being polite about it."
